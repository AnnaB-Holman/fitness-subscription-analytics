# Analyst Memo - Subscription Analytics
## An analysis of FitnessHub customer subscription data from 2024-2025 using Tableau

## Customer Cohort Analysis
Customers typically cancel their subscriptions after 3 months, as that is when the cohort numbers drop the most significantly.

## Revenue Forecast
The expected subscription revenue over the next twelve months is $320,616 by January 2027, which is a 165% increase from the current revenue of $193,614 for January 2026. The forecast shows no evidence of seasonality.

## CAC vs LTV
It takes until September 2025 for cumulative LTV to exceed CAC.
